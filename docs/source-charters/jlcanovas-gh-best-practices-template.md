## Declared intention and audience

The repository provides a collection of templates to kickstart a project, with
instructions to edit and adapt them to its needs. It addresses repository owners,
maintainers and contributors.

## GES interpretation: authority and limits

Use its repository-documentation and community-governance patterns as adaptable
starting points. Preserve the guidelines' must/should/could distinctions within
the source's own context; these words do not independently authorize GES policy.

Governance includes placeholder maintainers and specific response/review rules:
[governance, lines 7–43](https://github.com/jlcanovas/gh-best-practices-template/blob/bf13cd2c7992876da01de065084e8af3e9c7db06/GOVERNANCE.md#L7-L43).
Identities, deadlines, reviewer counts and veto procedures require local owner
decisions. Template text grants no real GitHub permission. Linked references are
not silently added to the founding source corpus; licensing requires separate
review, not inference from the template's presence.

## Synthesis obligations

Identify placeholders and parameterize local decisions. Distinguish structural
template reuse from adoption of example governance policy. Keep source-internal
contradictions explicit for later reconciliation rather than resolving them by
copying whichever file is most convenient.
