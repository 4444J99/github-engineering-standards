### Accounting defects at the reviewed head

The adversarial review confirmed two defects at `dc799db` that the recorded
checks did not cover:

1. Publication accounting accepted a fabricated artifact row and matching local
   source bytes when the repository and commit match a pin. It does not prove
   that the artifact path, digest and identity belong to that locked source
   inventory. The repair checks every complete per-pin artifact group against
   A3's independently bound count and content-identity digest before source uses
   can be evaluated. Canonical fabricated IDs, matching RAW or snapshot bytes,
   and rebound synthetic approvals cannot bypass that check.
2. `prepare-draft` accepted a new draft directory inside the candidate output
   root. It inventoried the output tree, wrote its draft metadata into that
   tree, then left accounting to reject those additional files. The repair
   refuses equal, nested, normalized relative and symlink-alias destinations
   before the inventory walk. A disjoint sibling destination remains usable.
